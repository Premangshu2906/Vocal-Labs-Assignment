# Vocal-Labs-Assignment

Product Teardown: Vocallabs.ai

This is my product teardown submission for the Product Intern role at Vocallabs.ai.

Instead of just looking at the surface, I spent time hands-on with the platform to find real, actionable opportunities. This teardown breaks down what's working and what can be improved across Product Features, UX/UI, Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy, and building user trust.

Areas Covered
GTM & ICP Analysis - Looked at how the product positions itself, who it is mainly targeting, and whether the messaging feels clear for different types of users.
UX & Conversion Friction - Breaking down the onboarding flow to find where users get stuck or confused.
Trust & Social Proof - Evaluating how the platform builds credibility—looking at testimonials and things that boost buyer confidence.
Product-led Growth Opportunities - Analyzed how easily users can experience the product before needing to speak with the customer helplines.
Multilingual Positioning: Checking how well the platform highlights its "Built for Bharat" and multilingual capabilities.
